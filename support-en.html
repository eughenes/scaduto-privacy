Scaduto! · Support

How can we help?

Find practical guidance and report a reproducible problem.

Report a problem

Search existing issues first. If the problem is new, open a GitHub issue; a GitHub account is required to submit it. You can describe the problem in your preferred language.

Open a GitHub issueBrowse existing issues

GitHub issues are public. Do not attach inventories, backups, invitation links, passwords, personal or health information. Share screenshots only after removing identifying or sensitive details.

What to include

  • App version and build, shown in Settings → About, privacy and licences.
  • Device model and iOS, iPadOS or macOS version.
  • App language and device region, particularly for dates or nutrition values.
  • Whether storage is local, personal iCloud or a family archive; include the sync status.
  • Steps to reproduce, expected behavior and actual behavior.

Before reporting

The list is not up to date

Check the cloud status on the main screen and use Update now. Offline changes remain on the device and transfer when the connection and iCloud are available. Do not uninstall the app to troubleshoot: export local data you want to keep first.

I cannot join a family archive

Accept the owner’s CloudKit invitation with the Apple account used on that device. Apple Family Sharing membership alone does not connect the archive. The owner should check participant status and permissions.

A label or date is not recognized correctly

Use good lighting and include the full date, or the nutrient names, numbers, units and basis. Review recognized values against the packaging; manual entry remains available.

I am not receiving reminders

Check notification permission in the device settings and the product’s saved reminder. The scheduled time must still be in the future; saving a reminder does not grant notification permission.

How do I keep a backup?

In Settings, open Backup, import and export. Export a copy before replacing data or removing the app. Keep backup passwords safely: the developer cannot recover them.

Privacy or security concerns

Contact privacy@vacchouse.com privately for privacy questions or security concerns. The developer cannot access or delete your device or iCloud inventory.

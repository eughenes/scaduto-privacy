Privacy

Scaduto! privacy information

Updated 29 October 2026.

Information managed by the app. Scaduto! stores the information you enter: products and subscriptions, categories, dates, notes, prices, nutrition facts and photos. Medicine and sensor names and notes may contain personal or health information. No app account is required.

Device storage and iCloud. The app keeps a local copy. If iCloud Drive is available, your inventory connects automatically to your iCloud storage; you can choose Local only in Settings. Preferences can sync separately using iCloud. Apple manages the service and account; the app does not receive your Apple password. The developer does not run a server that receives your inventory. Cloud protection depends on Apple's services and your settings; the app does not add its own end-to-end encryption layer to the iCloud inventory.

Scanning and photos. Text, date and barcode recognition uses Apple frameworks on your device. Scanner frames are not sent to an external OCR service. Photos you choose to save become part of the inventory and sync when cloud storage is enabled. Thumbnails are resized and re-encoded without retaining original metadata such as GPS location.

Food lookups. When you scan or enter a food barcode that is not found locally, the app may look it up automatically using Open Food Facts. Requests contain the barcode and technical information such as app name/version and language. The service also receives the IP address needed for the connection. Product images may be downloaded from Open Food Facts services. The app does not upload your photos, notes, expiry dates or medicine information to Open Food Facts. The provider handles requests under its own privacy policy.

Online search choice. Online search is initially off. Before the first external search, choose whether to allow it. Change this device-only choice in Settings → Privacy; it does not sync through iCloud.

Family archive. Family sharing uses Apple CloudKit invitations, independently of Apple Family Sharing group membership. Authorized participants can read products, notes and photos; participants with editing permission can change or delete them. Before switching you choose whether to use the shared data alone or add this device’s products. Share only information you want every participant to see, including medicine and sensor information. Stopping sharing keeps the owner’s archive as personal iCloud data; leaving can keep a local copy. Revoking access does not erase downloaded or exported copies. The selected archive is remembered privately in the same Apple account for other installations; another account must accept its own invitation.

Notifications. Reminders are scheduled locally and require your permission. Medicine and sensor names are hidden in notifications by default. You can change this preference and reminder times in Settings.

Backups and sharing. Exported files contain products, notes and photos. You can protect them with a password. The password is not stored or recoverable by the developer. Anyone receiving an unprotected file can read it. Sharing through Files or other apps uses the services you choose and their terms. Deleting an item in the app does not delete copies you previously exported or shared.

Retention and deletion. Deleting a product removes it from the list. Earlier data, catalogue entries and photos may remain in history and backups. Deletion records are retained after compaction so offline devices cannot bring deleted items back. To remove all data, stop sharing and choose Local only on every connected device, delete any Scaduto! folder retained in iCloud Drive, manage the app’s CloudKit data through Apple’s iCloud storage controls, and remove local and exported copies you no longer want. CloudKit archives are not folders in Files; stopping sharing or deleting the Drive folder alone does not erase them. New automatic safety copies are limited to the latest ten; manual backups can be removed from the saved backups screen. Recovery copies of unreadable archives remain in the app's private storage until uninstall. Uninstalling removes the app's local copy, not the iCloud inventory or exported files. Manage those separately through Files and Apple's settings.

Advertising and analytics. The app's code includes no advertising, tracking tools or external developer analytics services. External service requests and any diagnostics you provide to Apple are also subject to those services' privacy information.

App developer and contact. Scaduto! is developed by Eugenio Vacca. Use the address below for information, access or deletion requests about data you send to support. The developer cannot access or erase your local or iCloud archive: use the app and Apple controls described above. Contact. For privacy questions, email privacy@vacchouse.com.
